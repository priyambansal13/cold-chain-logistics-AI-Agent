# SOP-DS-006: Dispatch Action Catalogue and Approval Workflow

Owner: Control Tower Operations · Revision 2 · Effective 2026-06-01

## 1. Action types
| Action type | Use |
|---|---|
| MONITOR | Increased monitoring cadence; no physical intervention |
| PRE_COOL | Lower reefer setpoint ahead of a heat zone (SOP-WX-003) |
| REROUTE | Change route or move travel to night hours |
| DIVERT_COLD_STORAGE | Divert to a cold-storage facility for cross-dock or holding |
| MAINTENANCE_DISPATCH | Send a mobile reefer technician |
| CUSTOMER_NOTIFY | Inform consignee of deviation, delay or risk |
| ESCALATE_QA | Open a QA deviation / escalation |
| QUARANTINE | Place cargo on QA hold at delivery |

## 2. Priorities
- P1 Critical: imminent or active product loss; act within 15 minutes.
- P2 High: projected breach within 2 hours or a major excursion already recovered.
- P3 Routine: preventive and informational actions.

## 3. Approval workflow
Actions proposed by the AI copilot are created with status PROPOSED and must be approved by a human dispatcher before execution. The copilot must not duplicate an existing PROPOSED or APPROVED action of the same type for the same shipment. Each action must reference the SOP sections that justify it.
