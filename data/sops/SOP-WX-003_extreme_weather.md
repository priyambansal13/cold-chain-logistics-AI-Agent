# SOP-WX-003: Extreme Weather and Heatwave Operations

Owner: Network Planning & Dispatch · Revision 4 · Effective 2026-05-10

## 1. Purpose
Reefer units lose capacity as ambient temperature rises. Route weather must be checked for every in-transit shipment whose remaining route has forecast threats. This SOP defines weather threat levels and the mandatory mitigations.

## 2. Weather threat levels (heat)
| Forecast max ambient on remaining route | Level | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| 30 °C to 34.9 °C | ELEVATED_HEAT – Level 1 | All classes |
| 35 °C to 39.9 °C | SEVERE_HEAT – Level 2 | All classes |
| 40 °C and above | EXTREME_HEAT – Level 3 (heatwave) | All classes |

## 3. Heatwave mitigations by level
- Level 1 (Elevated): Increase monitoring to every reading; confirm reefer fuel ≥ 50%; no mitigation action required.
- Level 2 (Severe): Pre-cool the reefer 1 °C below nominal setpoint (PRE_COOL action) before entering the heat zone, but never below the lower range limit. Avoid stops longer than 20 minutes between 12:00 and 17:00 local time. Door openings are prohibited except at destination. For FRESH_PRODUCE and PHARMA, notify the customer of a possible delay (CUSTOMER_NOTIFY).
- Level 3 (Extreme / heatwave): All Level 2 mitigations PLUS, for FROZEN, DEEP_FROZEN and PHARMA cargo: reroute to avoid the hottest segment if an alternative adds less than 3 hours, OR schedule night travel (REROUTE action). If the reefer is already HIGH_LOAD or DEGRADED, divert to the nearest compatible cold-storage facility and wait out peak heat (DIVERT_COLD_STORAGE action).

## 4. Other weather threats
- HEAVY_PRECIPITATION (≥ 25 mm/day) or THUNDERSTORM: expect delays; notify customer if projected ETA slips by more than 2 hours.
- SNOW_OR_ICE: chain-up rules apply; for PHARMA cargo ensure heating mode is enabled to prevent freezing below +2 °C.
- EXTREME_COLD (≤ -15 °C): CHILLED, PHARMA and FRESH_PRODUCE are at freeze risk; verify reefer heating mode is active.
- HIGH_WIND (≥ 60 km/h): high-sided vehicle restrictions; dispatcher to evaluate route closures.
